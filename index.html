Sivitor – Smart Visitor Management

Privacy Policy
Effective Date: July 28, 2026

Introduction

Sivitor – Smart Visitor Management is a visitor and security management application developed and operated by Srivyn Platforms Pvt Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect your information when you use the Sivitor mobile application, website, and related services.

Information We Collect

  • Personal information such as name, email, phone number, address, profile photo.
  • Visitor information including visitor name, purpose, host, check-in/check-out time, vehicle details, visitor photo, and ID details where required.
  • Resident and employee information for approvals and visitor management.
  • Device information, IP address, app version, operating system, crash logs.
  • Location information (with permission) for security and attendance features.
  • Camera access to capture visitor photos and scan QR codes.
  • Notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver visitor alerts and approvals.

How We Use Information

  • Register and manage visitors.
  • Send approval requests and security notifications.
  • Create digital gate passes and QR codes.
  • Maintain visitor history and security logs.
  • Improve application security and performance.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Permissions

  • Camera
  • Location
  • Storage/Photos
  • Notifications
  • Internet

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your information. Information may be shared with organizations using Sivitor, trusted cloud providers, notification providers, and authorities when legally required.

Data Security

We use encryption, access controls, secure cloud infrastructure, monitoring, backups, and industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

Your Rights

  • Access your data.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where permitted.
  • Withdraw consent when applicable.
  • Request a copy of your data.

Third-Party Services

Sivitor may use Firebase Cloud Messaging, analytics, and secure cloud hosting providers to deliver services.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13.

Changes to this Policy
